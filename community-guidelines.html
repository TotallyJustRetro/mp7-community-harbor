KEEP THE HARBOR FRIENDLY

Community Guidelines

These rules are a starting point for the fan community. The site owner should review and adapt them before enabling public accounts or uploads.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, hate, threats, or targeted abuse.
  • Keep feedback constructive and focused on creations.
  • Do not impersonate other creators, moderators, or companies.

Protect personal information

  • Do not post home addresses, phone numbers, private emails, passwords, or other personal information.
  • Use a screen name rather than your real name.
  • Do not pressure other members to move conversations to private channels.

Only share content you have permission to share

  • Upload only boards, mods, models, textures, music, and images you created or are allowed to redistribute.
  • Do not upload Nintendo game files, ROMs, executable files, malware, or content that violates someone else's rights.
  • Do not disguise an executable or script inside a board package.

Moderation

New board packages are private and pending review until approved. Reports are reviewed by the site moderation team. Content may be rejected or removed for safety, rights, compatibility, or rule violations.

Unofficial project

MP7 Community Harbor is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo.
