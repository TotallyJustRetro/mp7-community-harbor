YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice — Draft

Important: this is a project starter notice, not legal advice or a final privacy policy. The site operator must confirm the actual services, retention periods, support contact, and applicable requirements before enabling public registration.

Information the site may process

  • Account email address, handled by the authentication provider for sign-in and verification. It is not displayed on public creator listings.
  • Public display name and board metadata such as title, description, supported player counts, tags, and upload date.
  • Board package files submitted for moderator review.
  • Reports submitted about community content.
  • Basic technical information processed by the hosting and backend providers to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, display approved community creations, deliver board packages, prevent abuse, and moderate reported content.

Third-party services

The planned deployment uses Cloudflare Pages for website hosting and Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Their own privacy and service terms also apply. The operator should link and review the providers' current policies before launch.

Public content

Display names, approved board descriptions, tags, and approved downloads are visible to site visitors. Do not include personal contact details in public content.

Contact and deletion requests

Before opening registration, the site operator must publish a monitored contact method and a process for account/content deletion requests. This draft intentionally does not invent a contact address.

Launch checklist

  • Confirm the site operator and moderation contacts.
  • Set an appropriate account age policy and review applicable provider terms.
  • Decide how long rejected uploads and reports are retained.
  • Test account deletion and data removal procedures.
  • Replace this draft with a complete, accurate notice.
